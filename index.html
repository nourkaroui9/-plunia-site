/ 02 — The problem

Mid-surgery, the patient's imaging sits on a screen the surgeon cannot touch.

9.8
Distractions per hour in the operating room — driving surgical errors, infections and skipped safety checks. Antoniadis et al., J Surg Res, 2014
45%
Of surgeons report feeling distracted during the operation itself. Jung et al., Surg Endosc, 2020
$46–133
Cost of a single minute in a US operating room. Every imaging consultation today burns ten of them. Childers & Maggard-Gibbons, JAMA Surgery, 2018
/ 03 — The system

The instruments already in the surgeon's hands become the interface.

/ See
Real-time instrument recognition
Our AI model identifies the surgical instruments live in the endoscopic video — no markers, no sensors, no calibration step.
/ Map
Configurable interaction layer
Tool gestures map to interface actions through a reconfigurable layer — the core of our patented approach.
/ Interact
Imaging, inside the view
Preoperative MRI and CT are browsed as an image overlay on the live endoscopic feed — the sterile field stays intact.
Ten minutes of imaging consultation → one. Without anyone leaving the table.
